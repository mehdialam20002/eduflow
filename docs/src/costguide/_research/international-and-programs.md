# International Entity Costs, Startup Credits and Government Programmes (checked 21 September 2026)

Scope: what it costs EduFlow to operate in the UAE (Year 2), USA and Australia (Year 3 pilots), the Indian rules for owning a foreign subsidiary and exporting services, and every realistic credit, grant, loan or incubator programme for a bootstrapped solo founder in Patna / Lucknow / Delhi NCR.
Conversion: US$1 = Rs 85, A$1 = Rs 56, AED 1 = Rs 23. "Rs" figures are rounded. 1 lakh = 1,00,000; 1 crore = 100 lakh.
Tax notes: UAE free zone, Delaware and ASIC government fees carry no VAT/GST unless stated. Service-provider fees abroad may add local VAT (UAE 5%) or GST (Australia 10%). When the **Indian** company pays a foreign vendor, 18% IGST applies under reverse charge and is claimable as input tax credit once GST-registered (Estimate, standard GST rule). Fees paid by a foreign subsidiary carry no Indian GST. Indian government schemes below are grants, loans or guarantees, not prices, so GST does not apply to them; Indian CA/incubator fees carry 18% GST.
Status: **Verified** = read on the vendor, government or regulator page itself (WebFetch, 21 Sep 2026). **Estimate** = from a secondary source (law firm, adviser, aggregator, news) or reasoned; the reason is given. Mixed rows say which part is which. Source codes [S1] to [S103] are listed at the end.
Payment collection abroad (Stripe availability for Indian businesses, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy fees) is covered in `_research/messaging-payments.md`; it is only cross-referenced here.

## Differences from the business plan

The plan (BRD numbers in `_anchors.md`) stays as the Recommended level. These are the places where current prices or rules differ.

1. **No government grant money should be planned.** The BRD funds the company with Rs 6,00,000 founder capital and no grants, which is right: SISFS (Rs 20 lakh grant) closed to new applications on 31 May 2026. The realistic non-dilutive options are the Bihar seed loan (Rs 10 lakh, 0% for 10 years, only for a company registered and operating in Bihar) and the UP 2020/2026 policy money (only for a UP company working through a recognised incubator). The state of incorporation decides which one is open.
2. **Sentry (plan: Team at Rs 2,200 from December).** Sentry for Startups gives up to US$5,000 of credits for 12 months to companies under 2 years old that raised under US$5M. If approved, the six-month cost falls from Rs 8,800 to Rs 0 and Year 1 saves about Rs 26,000.
3. **Zoho Books Rs 750 and CRM Rs 250 a month from January.** After DPIIT recognition, Zoho for Startups gives Rs 1,86,000 of Zoho credits for 360 days. If the CRM is also Zoho, both lines can be Rs 0 in Year 1 (saves Rs 3,000 in six months, about Rs 9,000 in Year 1). Apply before paying for any Zoho product; paid history disqualifies.
4. **DPIIT recognition (plan: Rs 0).** Matches: recognition is free. The turnover limit is now Rs 200 crore (older guides say Rs 100 crore).
5. **Trademark (plan: Rs 4,500 government fee + Rs 3,000 attorney per class).** Matches the startup/small-entity fee, but only if the DPIIT or Udyam certificate exists at filing; otherwise the fee is Rs 9,000 per class (+Rs 9,000 for two classes). The SIPP scheme that paid the attorney ended on 31 Mar 2026, so the Rs 3,000 attorney fee stays.
6. **Claude Max (plan: Rs 17,000 a month).** No change. Anthropic startup credits need institutional equity funding, and they are API credits, not subscription credits.
7. **Vercel Pro (plan: Rs 1,700 a month).** No change. Vercel for Startups needs recent funding and a partner.
8. **AWS S3/SES (plan: Rs 2,700 in six months) and the later AWS move.** AWS Activate Founders (up to US$5,000, bootstrapped allowed) and Microsoft (US$5,000) are available but not counted in the plan. Keep them out of the budget as upside; claim AWS credits just before the move to AWS so they do not expire unused.
9. **Income tax (plan: 25% of positive EBITDA).** The section 140 (old 80-IAC) holiday can make 3 consecutive years tax-free for a DPIIT startup with an IMB certificate, incorporated before 1 Apr 2030. The plan's Year 2 and Year 3 tax (Rs 3 lakh and Rs 21 lakh) could be Rs 0. Keep the plan number until the certificate is granted.
10. **"UAE entry needs a local entity" (reasons to raise).** Partly true. Selling from India to VAT-registered UAE schools works without an entity (LUT in India, reverse charge in the UAE). A foreign seller has no VAT threshold, so non-registered customers force FTA registration from the first dirham, and Stripe needs a local entity. A UAE entity costs Rs 2.3 lakh (minimum) to Rs 8.35 lakh (Dubai, 1 visa) in the first year, well inside Year 2's Rs 25 lakh "legal, audit, office, insurance, travel, foreign entities" line and the Rs 48 lakh UAE share of the seed round.
11. **UAE corporate tax.** Small Business Relief now runs to 31 Dec 2029, so a UAE subsidiary with revenue up to AED 3 million pays 0% CT through Years 2 to 4. The plan adds no UAE tax, which stays correct.
12. **Gateway fees abroad (plan: 2.0% of revenue from Year 3).** California and Colorado start taxing SaaS on 1 Jan 2027, so a US pilot needs Stripe Tax (0.5% per transaction where registered) on top of card fees. US all-in cost per payment is about 4.5% (see `messaging-payments.md`), above the 2.0% plan average; the blended figure holds only while India dominates revenue.
13. **Foreign entity running cost (plan: inside G&A, Rs 60 lakh in Year 3).** Computed first-year cost of all three entities plus the India-side filings is about Rs 27 lakh (UAE 8.35 + USA 6.33 + Australia 9.02 + India 3.6); yearly after is about Rs 15 lakh. Fits, but Australia's resident director (A$6,600 a year) is the largest single recurring item.
14. **ODI limit.** A foreign subsidiary can receive at most 400% of the Indian company's net worth in its last audited balance sheet. With about Rs 5.5 lakh net worth after Year 1 (Rs 6 lakh capital minus the Rs 0.53 lakh loss), the limit is about Rs 22 lakh: enough for a UAE free zone company in Year 2, tight for three subsidiaries in Year 3 unless Year 2 profit is retained.
15. **Canon incubator note.** IIM Lucknow's incubator (IIML-EIC) runs from the Noida campus, not Lucknow city. For Lucknow, the StartinUP route (AKTU Innovation Hub, 6 months free) is the local option; for Patna, IIT Patna IC and BIA VenturePark.
