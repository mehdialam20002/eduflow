# Company, Legal, Tax and Compliance Costs in India for a Bootstrapped SaaS Startup (checked 21 September 2026)

Scope: what it costs EduFlow to exist as a legal business in India and stay compliant: choosing and registering the entity (Bihar, Uttar Pradesh or Delhi), bank account, GST, trademark, Udyam and DPIIT, income tax, yearly company-law filings, accountant and auditor, legal documents, security tests and certificates, cyber insurance, and the legal cost of a later seed round.

Conversion: US$1 = Rs 85, A$1 = Rs 56, AED 1 = Rs 23. "Rs" figures are rounded. "+GST" means 18% Indian GST is added on top; a GST-registered company can normally claim it back as input tax credit (ITC). Government fees (MCA, IP India, stamp duty) carry no GST. Foreign (USD) prices exclude Indian GST; a GST-registered company pays 18% IGST on them under reverse charge and claims it back.

Status: **Verified** = read on the government page or on the vendor's own price page with WebFetch on 21 Sep 2026 (URL in the Sources list). **Verified\*** = read on the official or vendor page in the first pass of this research on 21 Sep 2026, but that URL was not saved in the draft and the page could not be reopened in the second pass (domain or search limits); re-open before quoting externally. **Estimate** = from a secondary source (cross-checked where possible) or reasoned. Several official sites (mca.gov.in, incometaxindia.gov.in, pib.gov.in, vakilsearch.com, the Protean/NSDL PAN site) refused or failed automated access, so most MCA fee and tax-rate rows are marked Estimate even where several secondary sources agree. Source codes: S1 to S60 = opened in this pass; E1 to E12 = first-pass sources whose URLs were not saved; SR = search-result listing only (page not opened).

Legal note: from 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act. Old section 115BAA (22% option) is now section 200; old section 80-IAC (startup tax holiday) is now section 140. Old names are kept in brackets because most guides still use them.

## Differences from the business plan

1. **Company registration: plan Rs 20,000, market Rs 7,000 to 18,000.** Online platforms cost Rs 7,000 to 12,000 all-in; a local CA costs Rs 11,000 to 18,000. The plan is safe. The first pass missed the Rs 155 PAN/TAN fee inside SPICe+; it is now included.
2. **Authorised capital is not in the plan.** The MCA fee is zero only up to Rs 15 lakh authorised capital, and only at incorporation. Raising capital later costs Rs 15,000 to Rs 1.7 lakh (SH-7). The plan's example has 10 lakh shares before the seed round and 12.5 lakh after. With a Rs 1 face value and Rs 15 lakh authorised capital, that fits with no SH-7 fee. With a Rs 10 face value it would cost about Rs 1.7 lakh or more.
3. **Trademark: plan Rs 15,000 (Rs 4,500 government fee + Rs 3,000 attorney fee a class).** The Rs 4,500 rate applies only if the company holds a Udyam or DPIIT certificate when it files. Otherwise a Pvt Ltd pays Rs 9,000 a class, and two classes cost Rs 18,000 + fees, about Rs 24,000. Online filing fees (Rs 1,899 to 1,999 a class) are below the plan's Rs 3,000. With DPIIT and a SIPP facilitator the professional fee can be Rs 0. Also budget Rs 3,000 to 7,500 for a likely examination-report reply. Renewal is Rs 9,000 a class every 10 years, with no small-entity rate.
4. **GST registration: plan Rs 2,000, market Rs 0 to 2,000.** Matches. Register at incorporation (voluntary). ITC on services bought before registration is generally lost. Without a GSTIN on foreign invoices, 18% OIDAR GST on the Claude plan (about Rs 3,060 a month) becomes a pure cost.
5. **Legal documents: plan Rs 25,000.** That is at the low end. One privacy policy alone is Rs 15,000 at an Indian firm, and DPDP-grade drafting is Rs 50,000 to 1 lakh. Rs 25,000 works only as a lawyer's review of template drafts (iubenda or Termly templates cost Rs 0 to 1,300 a month). Suggest Rs 40,000 to 75,000 before the May 2027 DPDP deadline.
6. **Security test: plan Rs 20,000 (freelance tester).** Credible manual web-app VAPT in India costs Rs 40,000 to 1.5 lakh, and vendors call sub-Rs 25,000 tests scan exports. Keep Rs 20,000 for the pre-launch check. Budget Rs 75,000 to 1.5 lakh for the first formal VAPT report that schools or partners will ask for (Year 1 or 2).
7. **CA retainer: plan Rs 5,000 a month.** Inside the Tier 2 range (Rs 3,000 to 8,000). In Delhi NCR it would be Rs 5,000 to 15,000. Add 18% GST, which is claimable.
8. **First audit, tax return and annual filings: plan Rs 35,000 in August 2027.** The market is Rs 16,000 + GST (online bundle) to about Rs 40,000 (local CA audit + ITR-6 + ROC). The plan is fine. Note the timing: for a company incorporated in October 2026, the first year ends 31 Mar 2027. AOC-4 is due about 30 Oct 2027 and MGT-7A about 29 Nov 2027, which falls in Business Year 2. The audit work can still be paid in August. DIR-3 KYC is now every 3 years (first due 30 Jun 2029), so there is no yearly KYC cost.
9. **Income tax: plan 25% of positive EBITDA.** The s.200 (22%) regime gives an effective rate of 25.168% on taxable profit. It is simpler and has no MAT, so the plan is close. The s.140 (80-IAC) holiday needs a separate board approval, which only about 1.8% of recognised startups hold. It cannot be combined with the 22% regime. MAT at 14% (now a final tax) still applies in holiday years. Do not count on the holiday in the plan.
10. **Angel tax: no longer a risk.** It was abolished for all investors from FY 2024-25. A valuation report (Rs 25,000 to 75,000+) is still needed for the seed round, and legal fees for the round are Rs 1.5 to 5 lakh at a boutique firm. The plan's seed use of funds has no line for them, so add them to Year 2 G&A.
11. **Items not in the plan:** (a) a Kotak or HDFC startup account needs a Rs 50,000 average balance after its waiver period; Axis offers zero balance for 24 months at its discretion. (b) Cyber insurance costs Rs 25,000 to 95,000 a year for Rs 50 lakh to 1 crore cover; add it from Year 2, when school groups ask. (c) SOC 2 (Rs 7 to 14 lakh in year one) and ISO 27001 (Rs 2 to 4 lakh in year one, Rs 4 to 6.5 lakh over 3 years) fit the plan's Rs 20 lakh seed allocation for compliance and security. (d) Professional tax applies only if the registered office is in Bihar.
12. **Pending change:** the Corporate Laws (Amendment) Bill 2026 may later exempt some small private companies from statutory audit. It is not law yet, so keep the audit budget.
