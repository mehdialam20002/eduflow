# Master Price List

**In simple words:** This appendix lists every price used in this guide in one place, so every chapter uses the same numbers. We checked each price on public vendor pages, government fee schedules and trusted secondary sources on 21 September 2026. Prices, taxes and government fees change often. Open the vendor page and confirm the price on the day you pay.

## How to read this list

Each group of costs has one section. Each section has one or more tables with the same seven columns.

- **Item** is what you buy. **Vendor and plan** is who sells it and which plan.
- **Price** is the price in the vendor's own currency, as the vendor shows it.
- **In Rs** is the same price in Indian rupees, rounded. Large amounts use lakh (Rs 1,00,000) and crore (100 lakh), written in full.
- **Unit** says how often you pay: one time, per month, per year, per message, per transaction.
- **Free tier or notes** gives the free allowance, the catch, or the range when sources disagree.
- **Status** says how sure we are, followed by a source key in curly brackets, for example {CL:S23}. The two letters name the research area and the number names the source. Every key is listed in *Sources* at the end of this appendix.

### Verified and Estimate

- **Verified** means we read the price on the vendor's own price page, or on the government or regulator page, on 21 September 2026.
- **Estimate** means the price comes from a secondary source (an adviser blog, a salary site, a cost-of-living site, news, or a search-result summary), or we worked it out. It also covers rows read on an official page whose web address was not saved.
- Every salary, rent and cost-of-living figure is an Estimate, even when the page was opened. They are averages of reports, not price lists.
- **(calc)** means we worked the figure out from other rows. **(reasoned)** means an informed planning estimate with no single source. **(search)** means we saw the figure only in a search-result summary and did not open the page.

Some official sites refused automated reading on the check date: MCA, the Income Tax portal, Naukri, AmbitionBox, Glassdoor, IFZA, Google Cloud's startup pages and ASIC's fee page. Rows that depend on them are marked Estimate. All five research areas were available for this list: company, legal and tax; software, hosting and AI; messaging and payments; people, marketing and living costs; and international entities with startup programmes.

### GST treatment

GST (Goods and Services Tax, the Indian tax on sales) changes the real cost of many rows. The rules below apply unless a row says otherwise.

| Price type | What it means | Can EduFlow claim the GST back? |
|---|---|---|
| Indian price with "+ GST" | 18% GST is added on top of the price shown | Yes, as input tax credit (ITC) once GST-registered |
| Indian price "incl. GST" | The 18% is already inside the price | Yes, if the invoice shows EduFlow's GSTIN |
| Foreign price in US$, A$ or AED | Shown without Indian GST. EduFlow pays 18% IGST itself under reverse charge | Yes, in the same month. Without a GSTIN on the invoice, the vendor charges 18% that cannot be claimed |
| Government fee (MCA, IP India, stamp duty, RBI) | No GST | Not applicable |
| Retail price (laptops, prepaid mobile, printing, LPG) | GST already inside the price | Yes for business purchases with a GST invoice |
| Fuel | Outside GST; state VAT inside the pump price | No |
| Individual health or term insurance | GST-free since 22 September 2025 | Not applicable |
| Group (employer) health insurance | 18% GST on top | Yes |
| Salaries and stipends | No GST | Not applicable |

> **Rule:** Register for GST when the company is formed, and enter the GSTIN on every vendor account. GST paid before registration is usually lost.
