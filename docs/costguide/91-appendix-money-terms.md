# Money Terms in Simple Words

**In simple words:** This appendix explains every money, tax, legal and SaaS word used in this guide, one line each, with an example from the EduFlow plan. It also fixes the rules the whole guide follows: the three spending levels, the six columns of every cost table, and how GST is shown. Read a term here the first time it confuses you, then go back to the chapter.

## How to read a term here

- Each row gives the term, its plain meaning, and where it appears in this guide.
- Example prices come from the *Master Price List* and the BRD plan.
- Tax, company-law and payroll terms are simplified. Confirm the real treatment with the CA.

## The three budget levels

Every cost in this guide is shown at three levels.

| Level | What it means | Who it fits |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum | Free tiers, founder does the work, only what law or code forces | No savings, income from a job or freelancing |
| Recommended | The BRD financial plan, and the default answer | Rs 6,00,000 of founder capital, full-time from Day 1 |
| Maximum | The sensible upper end; above it is waste, not safety | Extra cash, or a customer already waiting |

Recommended always equals the BRD number: Year 1 total spend of Rs 23,77,500. Minimum and Maximum are ranges around that plan, not new plans.

## The standard cost table

Every chapter prices items with the same six columns: **Item**, **When to pay**, **Minimum**, **Recommended**, **Maximum**, **Can you avoid or reduce it?**

| Item | When to pay | Minimum | Recommended | Maximum | Can you avoid or reduce it? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI coding subscription | Monthly, from Day 1 | Rs 1,700 (Pro tier) | Rs 17,000 a month | Rs 17,000 a month | Drop to the Rs 8,500 tier if cash is tight |
| Error tracking | Monthly, from Dec 2026 | Rs 0 (free plan) | Rs 2,200 a month | Rs 6,800 a month | Yes, the free plan covers 5,000 errors |
| Pre-launch security test | One-time, Dec 2026 | Rs 15,000 | Rs 20,000 | Rs 1,50,000 | No, not before taking paid customers |
| Audit, tax return, ROC filings | Yearly, first Aug 2027 | Rs 18,879 (bundle) | Rs 35,000 | Rs 60,000 | No, compulsory even with zero sales |

All four rows use the *Master Price List*. Recommended column: Rs 17,000 + Rs 2,200 = Rs 19,200 a month of tools, plus Rs 20,000 one-time and Rs 35,000 yearly.

> **Rule:** Indian prices are before 18% GST unless a row says "incl. GST". Once GST-registered, that 18% comes back as input tax credit, but only when the invoice carries the company GSTIN. Foreign prices use US$1 = Rs 85 and carry 18% IGST under reverse charge, also claimable. Government fees, salaries and fuel carry no claimable GST.

## Terms, A to C

| Term | Meaning in simple words | Example from this guide |
|---|---|---|
| Advance tax | Income tax paid in four instalments during the year | Starts only after EBITDA turns positive |
| AOC-4 | MCA form carrying the year's accounts | About Rs 400, due near 30 October |
| ARPA | Average revenue per account, per month | Year 1 target Rs 5,000 |
| ARR | Annual run-rate revenue, closing MRR times 12 | Rs 72 lakh at the end of Year 1 |
| Authorised capital | Highest share value the company may issue | Start at Rs 15 lakh, avoid a Rs 1,70,000 fee |
| Burn | Cash spent in a month minus cash collected | Rs 80,500 in October 2026 |
| CAC | Money spent to win one paying customer | Year 1 limit Rs 12,000 |
| Cash flow | Real money in and out of the bank, not profit | Rs 25.76 lakh closing cash in Year 1 |
| Churn | Share of paying customers who leave each month | Target under 3 percent |
| Convertible note | A loan that later turns into shares | Not used; the plan raises priced equity |
| Credits | Vendor money that cuts one vendor bill, then expires | AWS Activate, up to US$5,000 |
| CTC | Cost to company, the full yearly cost of one person | Rs 70,000 a month for the first engineer |
| Customer advance | Yearly fee taken now for service not yet given | Rs 20.29 lakh held at the end of Year 1 |

## Terms, H to M

| Term | Meaning in simple words | Example from this guide |
|---|---|---|
| Input tax credit | GST paid on purchases, set off against GST collected | 18 percent on tools comes back |
| ISO 27001 | International security certificate large buyers ask for | Rs 4 to 6.5 lakh over three years |
| Lakh and crore | 1 lakh is Rs 1,00,000; 1 crore is 100 lakh | Rs 3.6 crore ARR at Year 2 end |
| LTV | Total gross profit one customer gives before leaving | Must stay above four times CAC |
| LTV to CAC ratio | Lifetime value divided by cost to win the customer | Target above 4 to 1 |
| LUT | Free yearly GST letter allowing export without charging GST | File before the first foreign invoice |
| MAT | Minimum alternate tax, 14 percent of book profit | Only in the normal tax regime |
| MDR | The fee a merchant pays on one payment | UPI above Rs 2,000 costs 0.4 percent |
| Merchant of record | A vendor who sells for you and files the taxes | Paddle charges 5 percent plus US$0.50 |
| MGT-7A | Yearly MCA return of shareholders and directors | About Rs 400, due near 29 November |
| MRR | Monthly recurring revenue from subscriptions | Rs 6 lakh at the end of Year 1 |

## Terms, N to R

| Term | Meaning in simple words | Example from this guide |
|---|---|---|
| OIDAR | Rule making a foreign software seller charge you Indian GST | Rs 3,060 a month lost on Claude Max |

| Term | Meaning in simple words | Example from this guide |

| TDS | Tax cut at source when you pay salary, rent or a contractor | Filed quarterly by the CA |
| Udyam | Free MSME registration | Needed for CGTMSE loan guarantees |
| Unit economics | What one customer earns and costs | Rs 5,000 ARPA against Rs 12,000 CAC |
| VAPT | A paid expert attack test on the app | Rs 40,000 to Rs 1.5 lakh a test |

> **Tip:** Three numbers decide almost every spending question here: runway in months, gross margin, and CAC against LTV. If a purchase does not improve one of them inside 90 days, it belongs on the later list.
