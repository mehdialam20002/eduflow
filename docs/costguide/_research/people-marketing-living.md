# People, Marketing and Living Costs in India (checked 21 September 2026)

Scope: what EduFlow pays for people (salaries, interns, freelancers, statutory costs, hiring), marketing (ads, listings, events, print, content, telephony) and what the founder pays to live, in North India Tier 2 cities (Patna, Lucknow, Indore, Jaipur) and metros (Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Pune).
Conversion: US$1 = Rs 85, A$1 = Rs 56, AED 1 = Rs 23. "L" = lakh (Rs 1,00,000). "+GST" = 18% Indian GST is added on top. Salaries are gross annual CTC (cost to company) unless the row says per month.
Status: **Verified** = the price was read on the vendor's or government's own page (WebFetch or direct page read, 21 Sep 2026). **Estimate** = from a secondary source (salary aggregators, blogs, cost-of-living sites, news) or reasoned from those; the reasoning is in the notes. All salary and cost-of-living figures are Estimate by this rule, even when the page was opened.
Access note: AmbitionBox, Glassdoor, Naukri recruiter pages and Forbes India blocked automated reading (HTTP 403) on 21 Sep 2026. Salary rows therefore use Indeed India (job-post based) and PayScale India (survey based), which were opened. Source codes are listed at the end.

## Differences from the business plan

1. **Onboarding and customer success executive (plan: Rs 30,000 a month from April 2027).** The market for a 1 to 3 year onboarding executive is Rs 20,000 to 30,000 a month in Tier 2 and Rs 30,000 to 50,000 in metros. The plan sits at the top of the Tier 2 range: fine in Patna or Lucknow, entry-level only in a metro. With fewer than 10 staff, no PF, ESI, gratuity or bonus is mandatory; add group health cover of about Rs 400 to 700 a month including 18% GST.
2. **Inside sales executive (plan: Rs 25,000 during a 3-month ramp).** Inside the Tier 2 fixed range of Rs 18,000 to 30,000. The plan shows no incentive; the market adds 10% to 30% of fixed pay, so budget about Rs 3,000 to 7,500 a month after the ramp.
3. **Full-stack engineer (plan: Rs 70,000 a month = Rs 8.4 lakh a year from 16 Aug 2027).** Above Tier 2 software averages (Rs 5.0 to 6.6 lakh in Lucknow, Indore, Jaipur) and inside the metro mid-level range (Rs 8 to 15 lakh). It buys a strong 3 to 5 year developer working remotely from a Tier 2 city. A senior (6+ years) costs Rs 9 to 16 lakh in Tier 2 and Rs 15 to 28 lakh in metros.
4. **Part-time tele-caller (plan: inside the marketing budget).** Internshala telecalling stipends in Patna are Rs 12,000 to 18,000 a month full time, so a half-day caller costs about Rs 6,000 to 9,000 a month. Check that the January to March marketing lines hold this.
5. **EPF wage ceiling (plan built on Rs 15,000).** The Cabinet raised it to Rs 25,000 on 16 Sep 2026. It does not bind in Years 1 and 2 (under 20 staff), but from Year 3 the employer's PF, EDLI and admin cost per covered employee rises to at most Rs 3,250 a month (was Rs 1,950). With the 50% wage rule, staff up to about Rs 50,000 a month CTC reach the ceiling. For a Rs 80,000 sales person that is about 4% on top of pay; the Year 3 loaded costs can absorb it but should say so.
6. **Other statutory costs are not itemised in the plan's "loaded cost".** ESI applies from 10 staff (Year 2): 3.25% employer share on anyone earning up to Rs 21,000 gross. Gratuity also applies from 10 staff: accrue about 2.4% of CTC, and fixed-term staff now qualify after 1 year. Statutory bonus (8.33% minimum) applies from 20 staff (Year 3) for staff with wages up to Rs 21,000. Professional tax is nil in UP and Delhi and up to Rs 2,500 a year per employee in Bihar, Karnataka and Maharashtra (deducted from pay, not an employer cost).
7. **Group health insurance (not in the plan).** Rs 2,500 to 6,500 per employee a year for employee-only cover, Rs 6,000 to 12,000 with family, plus 18% GST.
8. **Year 1 marketing (plan: Rs 5,85,000).** A DIDAC India shell stall alone is about Rs 2,86,740 with GST, half the Year 1 budget, before travel and stand costs; skip it in Year 1. Regional school expos (about Rs 15,000 to 60,000) and educator summits (Rs 20,000 per vendor seat) fit better. 10. **Local travel and co-working passes (plan: Rs 6,000 a month from January 2027).** One open desk costs Rs 4,000 to 6,000 a month in Patna and Lucknow and Rs 6,250 to 6,499 in Noida; WeWork All Access Basic is Rs 9,999 + GST. Rs 6,000 pays for a Tier 2 desk or for local travel, not both. Work from home, use day passes (Rs 299 to 475) for meetings, and spend the rest on demo travel.
11. **Sales telephony (not in the plan).** A shared business number with IVR costs about Rs 1,400 a month (Knowlarity prepaid) or about Rs 2,000 a month (Exotel Dabbler). MyOperator Sedan at Rs 5,000 + GST is a Year 2 tool. Budget about Rs 2,000 a month from February 2027 in Tools or Marketing.
12. **Hiring costs (not in the plan).** Free routes (Indeed, Internshala free listing, one LinkedIn free post) plus a Naukri Hot Vacancy at Rs 1,650 or an Apna credit at about Rs 650 give about Rs 2,000 to 5,000 per Year 1 hire. Small; take it from Misc.
13. **Founder living cost (plan: notional Rs 50,000 a month, Rs 3,00,000 for six months).** A frugal founder spends about Rs 25,000 a month in Patna or Lucknow, Rs 40,000 in Noida and Rs 46,000 in Bengaluru. The plan figure is conservative in Tier 2 (a six-month reserve of about Rs 1,50,000 would do) and about right in a metro. Founder pay step one (Rs 30,000 from April 2027) covers Tier 2 living but not a metro. Buy term cover (about Rs 9,500 a year for Rs 1 crore) and health cover (about Rs 9,500 single, Rs 16,000 to 21,500 for a couple) before 5 Oct 2026; both are GST-free now.
14. **Year 2 loaded costs per person.** Customer success at Rs 35,000 fits Tier 2 executives (Rs 20,000 to 30,000 pay plus ESI, health and tools). Sales and marketing at Rs 55,000 covers a mix of Tier 2 inside sales (about Rs 25,000) and a sales manager (Rs 33,000 to 58,000) with incentives. Engineering at Rs 1,00,000 fits a mid-level developer at metro-remote pay (Rs 8 to 15 lakh) plus an AI seat.
